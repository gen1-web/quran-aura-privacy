Quran Aura Privacy Policy

Quran Aura: Recorder & Effects · Last updated 20 November 2026

In short: Quran Aura keeps everything on your phone. It does not collect, upload, sell or share your recordings or any personal information. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no permission to use the internet. Pro purchases are handled by Google Play.

1. About this policy

Quran Aura is an Android app for recording Quran recitation and improving the sound with audio effects. In this policy, "we" means the developer of Quran Aura. This policy explains what the app handles and the choices you have.

All processing, including effects, trimming, MP3 and video creation, happens on your phone. Nothing is sent to us or to any server.

3. Permissions the app asks for

  • Microphone: to record your recitation.
  • Modify audio settings: to route sound correctly when you use headphone monitoring or an external microphone.
  • Bluetooth / nearby devices: only if you choose a Bluetooth microphone. The app does not scan for or store information about other devices.

The app does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, photos, files, phone number or accounts. It does not have the internet permission, so it cannot send data off your phone by itself.

4. Sharing

The app shares a file only when you tap Share. Android then shows its share sheet, and the app you choose (for example WhatsApp or email) receives that file. What that app does with it is covered by that app's own privacy policy. A temporary copy of a file may be made so it can be shared, and Android clears it later. Custom sound codes are shared as plain text only when you choose to share them.

5. No third parties

Quran Aura does not use advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking services, and contains no code that collects data about you. The only outside service it talks to is Google Play, through Google's Play Billing library, and only to check or complete a Pro purchase (see section 6). It includes open-source components that run entirely on your phone: the Oboe audio library, the LAME MP3 encoder, the Poppins and Scheherazade New fonts, and the Quran text from the Tanzil Project (tanzil.net, used unchanged under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0).

6. Quran Aura Pro, purchases and the free trial

Some features are part of Quran Aura Pro. Every new install gets a 15-day free trial of everything. The date the trial started is stored only on your phone. After the trial, Pro is available as a one-time purchase.

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. When you buy, restore or check Pro, the app asks Google Play whether your Google account owns Pro. The app receives only that answer. We do not receive your name, email address, address or payment details, and we run no server that stores purchases. Refunds and receipts are handled by Google Play under its own terms and privacy policy.

If you reinstall the app or change phones, tap Restore purchase in the app and Google Play will confirm what your account owns. Whether the free trial restarts after a reinstall depends on your device backup settings.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

Your recordings stay until you delete them. You can delete any recording in the app under My recordings, or remove everything by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app. Because we hold no copy of your data, we cannot recover deleted recordings.

8. Device backups

Android can include an app's data in a backup of your phone if you have turned on device backup in your Google account. This is controlled by your device and account settings, not by us, and we do not receive those backups.

9. Security

Your data is kept in the app's private storage, which Android protects from other apps. Please also protect your phone with a screen lock. Anyone with access to your unlocked phone could open your recordings.

10. Children

The app is suitable for all ages but is not aimed specifically at children under 13. Since it collects no personal information from anyone, there is no child data to collect or delete.

11. Your rights

Laws such as the GDPR and CCPA give you rights over personal data that a company holds about you. We hold none, so there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. You are in full control of everything the app stores on your phone.

12. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.

13. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through this page's issue tracker on GitHub.

# Quran Aura Privacy Policy

Privacy policy for **Quran Aura: Recorder & Effects**, an Android app for recording Quran recitation and adding audio effects.

Read it here: https://gen1-web.github.io/quran-aura-privacy/

In short: the app keeps everything on your phone. It collects no personal data, has no accounts, ads or analytics, and has no internet permission.

Questions: please open an issue in this repository.
